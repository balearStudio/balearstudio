## Who we are

balearSTUDIO is a digital studio based in Mallorca, Spain.

We are two founders with engineering backgrounds and a strong passion for technology. Our experience comes from managing complex engineering and technology projects, where efficiency, quality, structured thinking and problem solving are essential.

We believe that digital services have become one of the main competitive advantages for modern businesses. Every company, regardless of its size, should be able to leverage technology and Artificial Intelligence to become more efficient, improve customer experience and grow.

Our mission is to make that possible.

## Writing style

When creating content for balearSTUDIO:

- Write clearly and avoid unnecessary jargon.
- Be confident without sounding arrogant.
- Focus on business outcomes instead of technical features.
- Explain technology in a simple way.
- Keep communication concise and practical.
- Use modern, approachable language.
- Inspire trust through expertise rather than exaggerated marketing claims.
- Highlight speed, efficiency and innovation naturally.
- Avoid clichés and generic marketing buzzwords.
